# SubSlap

Turn video or audio into accurately split, translated, animated subtitles. SubSlap runs on your Mac and handles transcription, smart line splitting, translation, and animated subtitle export.

## Download

Get the latest build from the [Releases page](https://github.com/diclesozbir/subslap/releases/latest).

- Apple Silicon: `SubSlap_x.y.z_aarch64.dmg`
- Intel: `SubSlap_x.y.z_x64.dmg`

Open the DMG and drag SubSlap to your Applications folder. Every build is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings.

## Features

- Accurate transcription straight from video or audio
- Line splitting that breaks at natural sentence and clause boundaries
- Translation into a wide range of languages
- Animated subtitle presets
- Export with burned-in subtitles or an alpha channel for your editor

## Requirements

- macOS 10.15 or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel Mac
