# SubSlap

![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-1.3.30-FFCC00)

**Turn any video or audio into accurately split, translated, animated subtitles.**

SubSlap runs on your Mac. It transcribes, splits lines where sentences actually
break, translates, and exports captions you can burn in or drop onto a transparent
layer.

[**Download for macOS →**](https://github.com/diclesozbir/subslap/releases/latest)

## What it does

- **Transcribe from video or audio.** Drop in a file and get a timed transcript. Run
  it locally on-device, or use your own API key for cloud speed.
- **Split where sentences break.** Lines end at natural clause and sentence
  boundaries instead of a fixed word count. Sentence Mode gives you one caption per
  sentence, with no mid-thought cuts.
- **Teach it your words.** A glossary keeps names, brands, and jargon spelled the way
  you want, and biases transcription toward them.
- **Translate.** Convert captions into a wide range of languages while the timing
  stays put.
- **Animate.** Style captions with animated presets and preview them over your source
  video.
- **Export your way.** Burn subtitles straight onto the video, or render them on a
  transparent alpha layer to drop into your editor. Progress updates live while it
  renders.

## Built for

- **Creators** who want fast, styled, accurate captions on their own videos.
- **Editors and motion designers** who need clean, transparent-background caption
  assets to drop into an edit.

## Download

Get the latest build from the [Releases page](https://github.com/diclesozbir/subslap/releases/latest).

- **Apple Silicon** — `SubSlap-arm64.dmg`
- **Intel** — `SubSlap-x64.dmg`

Open the DMG and drag SubSlap to your Applications folder. Every build is signed and
notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings.
